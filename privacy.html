Studs Up: Red Card Football privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Studs Up is made by Half Grid Games. This policy covers the game Studs Up: Red Card Football (Studs Up for short) on Android, iPhone and the web.

What the game itself keeps

Studs Up has no accounts and we do not run any servers that store information about you. Your settings, coins, career progress and items you own are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads (Android and iPhone apps)

The phone apps show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how the app and ads perform, such as crashes and ad views. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's advertising policies and privacy policy. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the full-screen ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought so the game can unlock it.

Online 1v1

If you choose to play an online match, your device connects directly to your friend's device. A connection service (PeerJS) briefly sees both devices' IP addresses to set up the match. No names, messages or other personal details are sent.

Sharing goals

When you tap "Share this goal", the replay video is made on your device and only goes where you choose to send it.

Children

Studs Up is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
