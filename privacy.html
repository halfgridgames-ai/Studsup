Studs Up: Red Card Football privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Studs Up is made by Half Grid Games. This policy covers the game Studs Up: Red Card Football (Studs Up for short) on Android, iPhone and the web.

Studs Up does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet while you play.

Any game settings, such as sound on or off or your chosen team, are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Studs Up is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
